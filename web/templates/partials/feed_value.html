Petersen method [stpierre2004]: solve for the corn and soybean meal that match the hulls' net energy (NEL) and crude protein, and price them [nrc2001, ams_3146, wasde_nov2026]. A minus sign means the hulls carry less protein than corn for the same energy, so some soybean meal would have to be added back. It is a reference check, not a price anyone has offered: it assumes corn and soybean meal are fairly priced and ignores fiber, minerals and the feeding limits under the dairy options. Use it to judge whether a dairy's offer is low.
